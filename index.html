Procedure

  1. Give the other player the room code. They press their button in one steady rhythm. Six even beats calibrate the machine.
  2. Ask the question on your call or chat, then press Space (or tap Question asked) the moment it has been asked.
  3. They answer without breaking the rhythm. The next 10 beats are measured and they see TRUTH or LIE.
  4. Read the full analysis here. They keep pressing while the machine recalibrates for the next question.
