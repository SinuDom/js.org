  • The analyser compares the beats you click right after the question with your own steady rhythm before it.
  • Slowing down, speeding up, uneven beats and hesitations all raise the stress index.

Procedure

  1. Subject clicks the left mouse button on the chart, in one steady tempo. Six even beats calibrate the machine.
  2. Ask the question out loud, then press Space the moment it has been asked.
  3. Subject answers without breaking the rhythm. The next 10 beats are measured.
  4. Read the verdict. Keep clicking: the machine recalibrates for the next question.
